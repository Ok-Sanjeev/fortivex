📜 Fortivex Rules & Strategy

1. Objective

Classic 1v1: The first player whose pawn reaches any square on the opponent's starting baseline wins.

King of the Hill (4P): The first player to reach the central crown square at (4,4) wins immediately.

2. Turn Actions

On your turn, you must take exactly ONE action:

  • Move your pawn: Move 1 square orthogonally (North, South, East, West). Jump over adjacent pawns!
  • Place a barricade: Place a 2-space wall (Horizontal or Vertical) between tiles.
  • Break a wall (Breaking mode): Spend 5 barricades from your inventory to destroy an existing wall. The rubble is permanently walkable!

3. The Fair-Play Rule

You may never completely block a player's path to their goal. At least one legal path must always remain open.
