FORTIVEX — Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

FORTIVEX is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is collected, stored, and protected in compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and international privacy regulations.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Player Identity: We generate an anonymous guest username (e.g. noob10101) and unique player ID for game matchmaking. No personal email or phone number is mandatory.
  • Gameplay & Ratings: We record match records, ELO ratings (+30 / -30 Elo transactions), move histories, win streaks, and achievements to calculate leaderboards and match you with balanced opponents.
  • Network & Diagnostics: Anonymous latency and connectivity status to power the server-authoritative reconnection system.
  • No Advertising Trackers: FORTIVEX does not sell, rent, or share player data with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

3. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full ownership of your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated match histories directly within the game application:

  1. Open the Profile tab in the navigation bar.
  2. Scroll to Account Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.

Alternatively, players may submit a deletion request via our API endpoint POST /api/auth/delete-account or by contacting support at support@fortivex.io. Upon confirmation, your profile, sessions, and identifiable match entries are immediately and permanently erased.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or FORTIVEX data safety, please reach out at:
